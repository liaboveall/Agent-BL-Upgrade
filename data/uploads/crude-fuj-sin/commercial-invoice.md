## Pricing Basis (原油计价条款)

```
Unit Price = ICE Brent (QP mean)  +  Murban grade differential

  • Quotational Period (QP): mean of ICE Brent quotations over the
    5 quotations from B/L date onwards.
  • ICE Brent reference (as of B/L date):          USD 105.00 / bbl
  • Murban grade differential (Fujairah-loaded):   USD  +2.10 / bbl
  • Provisional unit price:                        USD 107.10 / bbl
```

> **PROVISIONAL INVOICE.** Final price to be settled on the published QP mean per contract.
> Brent trades around USD 105/bbl with the Strait of Hormuz largely closed to merchant traffic. Barrels
> loaded at Fujairah bypass the Strait and command a security-of-supply premium.
